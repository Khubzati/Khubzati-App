# Post-Launch Roadmap Suggestions

## Next 30 Days
- Enforce idempotency keys across all write-heavy client flows.
- Add dispatch assignment scoring (distance, shift fit, acceptance history).
- Build KPI ETL pipelines into `kpi_daily_fact`.

## Next 60 Days
- Add franchise hierarchy and parent-child bakery controls.
- Add dynamic slot pricing and cancellation penalties.
- Add vendor SLA scoring and proactive churn-risk flags.

## Next 90 Days
- Multi-city balancing with zone saturation controls.
- Predictive inventory replenishment alerts.
- Finance anomaly detection (refund abuse, payout drift, dispute spikes).

## AI Features
- Demand forecasting by bread type, weekday, and zone.
- Auto-suggested production plans for bakeries.
- Fraud/anomaly scoring for refunds/disputes.
- Intelligent dispatch assignment recommendation.
- Smart collections assistant for payout aging.

## Automation Opportunities
- Auto-close stale disputes with policy guardrails.
- Auto-escalate stuck payouts and queue lag thresholds.
- Auto-broadcast inventory shortage notices to restaurants.
- Auto-generate daily vendor settlement drafts.

## Competitive Advantages to Double Down On
- Bakery operational depth (inventory + production + dispatch in one platform).
- Finance-grade reconciliation/audit trail for B2B trust.
- Multi-role UX and localized Arabic/RTL operations.
- SLA-aware logistics orchestration for high-repeat restaurant demand.
